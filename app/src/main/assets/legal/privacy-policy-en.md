# Nopalito Scan Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 08/19/2026

## 1. Controller and contact

This Policy explains how **Ruben Matias**, the controller of **Nopalito Scan**, processes personal data. Privacy and
support contact: **contact@nopalitoscan.org**. Place or country of the controller: **Mexico**.

## 2. What data we process

Nopalito Scan is designed to process most information locally on the device. Depending on the features the User uses, we
may process:

- **Account data:** email address, user identifier, authentication data and the metadata needed to keep the session.
- **User Content:** photographs, scans, PDFs, images, extracted texts and QR codes that the User selects or captures.
- **Temporary files:** files sent to the cloud service to run a requested conversion or processing.
- **Stored files:** documents and minimal metadata — for example, name, size, date and type — when the User signs in and
  authorizes storing them in the cloud.
- **Technical data and logs:** IP address (stored in plain text or as a one-way hash depending on the configuration),
  approximate country derived from the IP, user agent, Application version and platform, request identifier, endpoint,
  method, status and duration of each request, email and user identifier when signed in, push notification token when
  notifications are enabled, and business events (sign-in, upload, download, deletion, conversion and administrative
  actions).
- **Device permissions:** camera and storage or file picker, only when necessary for the chosen feature. Nopalito Scan
  must not declare permissions it does not actually request.

We do not intend to collect special categories of data. However, the documents the User scans may contain personal,
financial, health, official identification or other sensitive information. The User decides which documents they send or
store and should avoid uploading information that is not necessary.

## 3. How we use the data

We use the data to:

- capture, convert, improve, generate, preview, download and deliver documents;
- store, sync and delete files when the User requests and authorizes it;
- authenticate accounts, keep sessions and provide support;
- detect abuse, protect security, investigate incidents and maintain availability; and
- comply with legal obligations and respond to valid requests from authorities.

We do not sell personal data nor use User Content for advertising. We do not use the documents to train models or for
purposes other than providing the requested feature, unless the User gives separate and explicit authorization or the
law requires it.

## 4. What happens when the cloud is used

Local processing does not require sending the document to the Operator. If the User selects a conversion or cloud
processing feature, the file is transmitted via **HTTPS/TLS** to the Operator's infrastructure or its technical
providers to run that request.

The file uploaded for temporary processing is deleted when the operation ends: for preview conversions, the file and
all intermediate artifacts are removed as soon as the result is delivered; for batch conversions, the uploaded files
and working directories are deleted when the job finishes. Leftovers from failures or interruptions are automatically
cleaned within at most 1 hour (upload staging files) and 24 hours (conversion working directories). If the User is not
signed in, conversion results are kept under an anonymous technical identity and automatically deleted after 90 days.
QR codes generated by the User are published at public, immutable URLs and are not automatically deleted.

If the User signs in and authorizes storing the result, the document is kept in their account until they delete it or
request account deletion. The User can preview, download and delete it from the Application.

## 5. Providers and transfers

We may share data with providers that host servers, store files, deliver email, manage authentication, monitor errors or
protect the infrastructure. These providers may only process the data following the Operator's instructions and to
provide those services. In particular, providers may include: hosting and database (MySQL), local or S3-compatible
object storage (for example, Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, MinIO, Backblaze B2 or Wasabi depending on the configuration),
email delivery (SMTP), content delivery and protection (Cloudflare), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and
IP geolocation (ip-api.com, with a local country-level cache).

We do not share documents with advertisers or with third parties for their own commercial purposes. We may disclose
information if necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect rights and security, investigate fraud or respond to
an emergency.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep account data for as long as the account exists or as long as it is needed to provide the service. Stored files
remain until the User deletes them or requests account deletion.

Temporary processing files are deleted when the operation completes; leftovers from failures or interruptions are
automatically cleaned within at most 1 hour (upload staging files) and 24 hours (conversion working directories). Files
moved to the trash are permanently deleted automatically after **30 days**. Activity and security (audit) logs are kept
for **90 days**; conversion jobs are kept for **90 days**; session and refresh-token data is purged after **30 days**;
application logs are kept in rotating files (14–30 files of 10 MB); and IP geolocation data is cached for **30 days**.

## 7. User rights

Under applicable law, the User may request access, correction, portability, deletion, restriction or objection to the
processing, and may withdraw consent when processing is based on it. They may also file a complaint with the competent
data protection authority.

To exercise rights, write to **contact@nopalitoscan.org** from the address associated with the account and state what
you request. We will respond within the applicable legal period and may ask for reasonable information to verify your
identity.

## 8. Account deletion

Deletion must be able to be initiated from **contact@nopalitoscan.org**, without requiring the Application to be
reinstalled. Once the request is completed, we will delete the account, the stored files and the associated data, except
for data we must keep due to a legal obligation or for security; that data will be limited, protected and deleted when
the reason for retention ends.

Target completion period: **2 weeks**. Deleting the Application from the device does not automatically delete an account
or the data stored in the cloud.

## 9. Security

We apply reasonable security measures, such as encryption in transit, access controls, account separation, credential
management, security logs and data minimization. Sign-in uses two steps (password plus a one-time code sent by email),
access tokens are short-lived, sessions can be revoked, attempts are rate-limited, passwords are stored as one-way
hashes and logs filter sensitive values. Content is transmitted encrypted, but is not encrypted at rest on our
infrastructure; avoid storing documents that require a higher level of protection. No internet-connected system is
completely secure; the User must also protect their device and their credentials.

## 10. Children

Nopalito Scan is not directed at children under **8 years old**. We do not deliberately collect children's data without
the required legal basis or authorization. If you believe a child provided us with data, contact
**contact@nopalitoscan.org** to request its review and deletion.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the Application, the backend, the providers or the law. We will publish
the current version within Nopalito Scan and update the date. If a change requires consent, we will request it before
processing the data for the new purpose.

## 12. Acceptance

Before enabling features that require acceptance, Nopalito Scan will show a clear notice with links to this Policy and
to the Terms. The User must perform an affirmative action — tick the circular box and press "Accept"; closing the
notice, tapping outside it or pressing "Back" will not be considered acceptance.
